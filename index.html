What Is FNF Nocturnal Protocol?

FNF Nocturnal Protocol is a fan-made rhythm game mod for Friday Night Funkin' that recreates the Nocturnal Protocol song from the FNF: Myths of Tubbyland project. FNF Nocturnal Protocol pulls its dark visual style directly from the Slendytubbies horror series, delivering a terrifying rhythm battle unlike any other FNF mod.

FNF Nocturnal Protocol is built around a single high-intensity track at 125 BPM, progressing through five distinct phases: an isolated whistle intro, a creeping verse with deep 808 bass, a frantic chase sequence, a terrifying mutation climax at the 2:20 mark, and an outcome-dependent outro. Whether you play FNF Nocturnal Protocol on Easy or Expert, the Slendytubbies atmosphere makes every note feel like a fight for survival.

FNF Nocturnal Protocol — The 2:20 Drop

Tinky Winky's Mutation — The Most Iconic Moment in FNF Nocturnal Protocol

One of the most iconic moments in FNF modding history occurs at 2:20 in FNF Nocturnal Protocol. Tinky Winky suddenly undergoes a horrifying on-screen transformation — the legendary "2:20 Drop" that every FNF Nocturnal Protocol player remembers:

2:18

The music shifts to a jarring, escalating frequency

2:20

Tinky Winky's body contorts violently. Bones visibly rearrange. Pupils disappear

2:20–3:00

Note density surges dramatically. Screen shakes violently. Red-white visual filters pulse across the stage

In FNF Nocturnal Protocol, this sequence visualizes the pathogen infection process from Slendytubbies III: Noo-Noo's engineered pathogen, transmitted through contaminated Custard, causes violent and irreversible genetic recombination. The FNF Nocturnal Protocol mod recreates this biological horror through gameplay — the sudden spike in difficulty and visual chaos mirrors the pathogen erupting within Tinky Winky's body.

FNF Nocturnal Protocol — Dual-Ending Custard Notes

FNF Nocturnal Protocol features 10 special green-glowing Custard Notes scattered between 1:38–2:18. Your performance on these notes determines which of the two FNF Nocturnal Protocol endings you unlock.

FNF Nocturnal Protocol Good Ending

✅ Hit all 10 Custard Notes

✅ Maintain >95% overall accuracy

→ Guardian collects samples and successfully escapes the ruins

FNF Nocturnal Protocol Bad Ending

❌ Miss all 10 Custard Notes (0/10)

⚠ Keep health bar above zero on regular notes

→ Guardian is captured and assimilated by Tinky Winky

💡 How to Get the Good Ending in FNF Nocturnal Protocol

  • • Memorize Custard Note positions (1:38–2:18)
  • • Train anti-distraction focus during 2:20 chaos
  • • Maintain >95% overall chart accuracy

💀 How to Get the Bad Ending in FNF Nocturnal Protocol

  • • Precision miss: let every green Custard Note pass
  • • Hit all regular arrows with "Sick"/"Good" judgment
  • • Counter-intuitive but requires extreme precision
